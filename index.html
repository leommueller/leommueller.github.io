Leo Müller
Leo Müller (*1995 Berlin, DE)
ABOUT
is an architect (MSc ETH Arch) working between Zurich and Berlin. His practice spans architecture, spatial design, and research. It explores the histories embedded in existing buildings and the traces, layers, and contradictions that reveal architecture as a continuous process rather than a finished object.
